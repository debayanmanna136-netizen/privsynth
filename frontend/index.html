The Three-Phase Workflow

A structured methodology connecting raw data profiling to privacy-evaluated synthetic output.

01

Profile & Inspect

Upload your CSV tabular dataset. PrivSynth automatically calculates column schemas, null proportions, and displays an immediate data preview.

02

Synthesize

Train a Conditional Tabular GAN (CTGAN) or Gaussian Copula on your data distributions without sending sensitive records to external cloud APIs.

03

Audit & Export

Inspect multivariate fidelity, verify empirical privacy distances against the training set, preview synthetic rows, and export clean CSV datasets.

System Architecture

Modular generative models paired with empirical evaluation pipelines.

Deep Generative Synthesis

Leverages Conditional Tabular GANs with mode-specific normalization for multimodal continuous variables and log-frequency sampling for imbalanced categories.

CTGAN + Gaussian Copula

Statistical Fidelity

Evaluates pairwise correlation mean absolute error, two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov distances, and Jensen-Shannon divergence across all features.

Empirical Proximity Metrics

Measures normalized nearest-neighbour Euclidean distances between synthetic samples and real records to detect potential memorization risks.

Direct Streaming Export

Streams verified synthetic CSV files directly to disk for seamless integration into local ML model training scripts and analytics notebooks.

Research Framework & Integrity

PrivSynth investigates the fundamental trade-off between statistical utility and disclosure risk in tabular synthesis. Default hyperparameters (CTGAN, 25 epochs, embedding dimension 256) were selected based on Pareto frontier analysis over benchmark datasets including Adult Census and synthetic credit scoring.

Disclosure Notice: Empirical nearest-neighbour proximity metrics evaluate dataset distance heuristics. They do not constitute formal differential privacy guarantees and cannot mathematically eliminate inference risks on individual records.
